# Data safety for clinical users

Last verified: 2026-09-23

This pack may be used by a clinician, but it is not a substitute for organizational policy, data-processing agreements, or legal/compliance review.

- Do not place patient-identifiable information, medical record exports, credentials, or organization-restricted data in this repository.
- Do not assume a consumer AI subscription is approved for protected clinical data.
- For real clinical data, follow the institution's approved systems, contracts, retention rules, and access controls.
- When building clinical software, separate medical-content validation from software correctness. Verify clinical claims against appropriate primary sources/guidelines and software behavior against tests.
