Review the current main of atsushi-matsuoka/claude-knowledge, packs/openai-knowledge/. Read its README.md and AGENTS.md, then the matching skill only.

Choose a suitable way to use this pack in your actual environment. It contains openai-guide for product facts and gpt-workbench for improving GPT tasks. Do not assume either is already installed or that your current model can switch to GPT. Treat model entries as candidates, check source freshness and actual access, and state what you can and cannot execute.

Prefer concise on-demand guidance to loading all notes. Preserve the parent's Claude skills and the user's existing personal skills. Propose overlapping-skill ownership after reading real contents, not from names alone. Any implementation must stay within the user's authorization, be reviewed and tested, and not auto-merge or initiate paid evaluations. Report installation/read/activation/model invocation/effect measurement separately.
